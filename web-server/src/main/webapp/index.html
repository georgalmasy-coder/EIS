Systems Engineering

Unlocking Systems Engineering Excellence

EIS supports structured engineering work with focus on requirements, system breakdown, collaboration, traceability and product development overview.

EIS platform overview

  • Requirements management
  • System breakdown structure
  • Engineering change overview
  • Project and product traceability

1. Our SE Drivers of Change

Modularisation enables flexible designs, easier maintenance and faster upgrades. It allows complex systems to be maintained and improved without disrupting the entire product.

System of Systems thinking helps manage dependencies and interactions across connected subsystems, disciplines and stakeholders.

A strong product platform supports common components across variants, reducing complexity while accelerating innovation.

2. SE Product Maintenance

Engineering change management processes help ensure that modifications are handled in a controlled and transparent way.

Release planning supports coordination of product updates, technology roadmaps and engineering change requests.

Digital support for version control, documentation readiness and requirements traceability helps engineering teams in daily work.

3. SE Development

Operational concepts define system purpose, use scenarios and requirement context. This provides direction for design and validation.

Requirements structuring creates a clear foundation for architecture, development, verification and lifecycle management.

Functional, logical and physical architectures turn ideas into structured system models and actionable engineering work.

4. Collaboration and Benefits

Systems engineering creates a shared language between project teams, disciplines and systems engineers.

The benefits include improved validation, better design decisions, reduced time-to-market and improved product quality.
